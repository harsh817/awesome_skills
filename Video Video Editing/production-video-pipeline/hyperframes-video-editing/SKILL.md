# Hyperframes Video Editing

Use this skill when a video needs final assembly, editorial polish, motion graphics, proof sheets, or render validation through Hyperframes.

## Role Of Hyperframes

Hyperframes is the final editor. Use it to turn generated assets into a coherent video:

- Timeline structure.
- Captions and titles.
- Overlays and callouts.
- Pointer labels.
- Scene transitions.
- Audio placement.
- Proof frames and contact sheets.
- Final MP4 render.

## Editing Rules

- Keep the timeline explicit with start times and durations.
- Preserve continuity between generated clips with still bridges, shared backgrounds, or matching subject placement.
- Use overlays to explain; do not use overlays to hide major generation failures unless that is acceptable for the creative style.
- If a section is weak, inspect frames around that timestamp before editing.
- Keep viewer-facing labels polished and remove internal terms.

## Validation

Before final delivery:

- Run the Hyperframes checker.
- Confirm layout has no issues.
- Confirm runtime has no errors.
- Confirm contrast passes.
- Render the final MP4.
- Generate proof frames/contact sheets for changed or risky sections.

## Delivery

Report:

- Final MP4 path.
- Proof sheet path.
- Render duration and output details if available.
- Validation result.
