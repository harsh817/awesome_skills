# Fashion Visual Direction

Use this skill for fashion videos, outfit explainers, styling mistakes, dress analysis, body-shape guidance, or garment callouts.

## Creative Standard

Fashion output should feel premium, intentional, and useful. The viewer should understand why a look works, not only see a pretty outfit.

## What To Watch

Pay attention to:

- Silhouette.
- Waist placement.
- Fabric drape.
- Volume balance.
- Neckline and shoulder shape.
- Lengthening or shortening visual lines.
- Fit problems caused by tight, tucked, bulky, or overly stiff pieces.
- Whether labels or overlays block the face, garment, or main silhouette.

## Callout Language

Use short, viewer-facing phrases:

- Soft drape skims stomach.
- Clean waist keeps length.
- Wide leg balances frame.
- Long line, less break.
- Softer upper half.
- Volume needs structure.

Avoid internal labels such as draft names, version numbers, production pass labels, or workflow names in the final viewer-facing video.

## Visual Rules

- Keep the model's face and full outfit readable whenever the garment is being explained.
- Use pointer lines, dots, and compact labels for garment explanation after the final look lands.
- Do not over-label; three callouts is usually enough for a final outfit explanation.
- Keep fashion copy concrete and visual, not abstract.
