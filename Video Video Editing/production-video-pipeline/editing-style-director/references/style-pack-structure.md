# Editing Style Pack Structure

Use a style pack when an edit language should be reused across future videos.

## Folder/Reference Arrangement

Keep style packs under `editing-style-director/references/`.

Suggested naming:

- `style-pack-structure.md` for this guide.
- `<style-name>.md` for each reusable style.

Each style pack should include:

- When to use it.
- When not to use it.
- Visual base.
- Cut rhythm.
- Shot grammar.
- Overlay system.
- Typography system.
- Proof devices.
- Audio relationship.
- Asset requirements.
- Quality checks.

## First Style Pack: Studio Diagnosis Fashion

Use for fashion, styling, beauty, body-shape, outfit, grooming, or transformation explainers where a real human subject is visually diagnosed and improved.

Visual base:

- Clean vertical studio background.
- Real human model or realistic human visuals as the main subject.
- Restrained palette with one warm highlight color.
- Minimal environment so diagnosis overlays are readable.

Cut rhythm:

- Hook in the first 1-2 seconds.
- Hold the model long enough for viewers to inspect the outfit or body shape.
- Use quick overlay beats for diagnosis.
- Use a final reveal hold for payoff.

Shot grammar:

- Full-body or clear waist-up human subject.
- Before/after or option comparison.
- Clone or side-by-side comparison when showing alternatives.
- Close-up insert panels for fabric, fit, detail, product, or photo crop.
- Final styled reveal with callouts.

Overlay system:

- Red X or mistake mark for wrong choice.
- Pointer labels for corrected details.
- Boxes or zoom windows for close-up proof.
- Heat map or highlighted zone only when diagnosing attention/problem areas.
- Keep overlays explanatory, not decorative.

Typography:

- Short readable captions.
- Emphasis words highlighted in the accent color.
- Labels should be concrete: "clean waist", "soft drape", "bad angle", "styled DP".
- Avoid internal production names on screen.

Proof devices:

- Show the problem, not just say it.
- Show the fix next to the problem when possible.
- Use final callouts to explain why the improved look works.

Quality checks:

- Human subject must be credible and visible.
- Main face/outfit/body area must not be covered by text.
- The viewer should understand the transformation from the visuals even with audio muted.
